Reglazing Questions Answered

What is the difference between reglazing and replacing a bathtub?

Reglazing renews the surface of your existing tub, while replacement removes the tub entirely. Reglazing is usually quicker and less disruptive, especially in older buildings.


How long does a refinished tub take to cure?

Curing times depend on the materials and conditions. We tell you exactly when it is safe to use the tub and how to care for the new finish.


Can you reglaze wall tile as well as the tub?

Yes. We refinish bathroom wall tile and other tile surfaces, which can match the new tub for a fresh, consistent look.


Can you change the color of my tub or tile?

In many cases, yes. White is the most common choice, and we can discuss other options during your quote.
